How It Works

Whichever room drinks the most of a given game by the end of Sunday wins that game's point. There are 10 points up for grabs: the 5 games (with the Swim-Up Bar Circuit split into 5 separate bar contests), plus one bonus point for whichever room logs the most drinks overall, across everything combined.

Keeping It Honest

Every log shows up on the live feed with a timestamp. If something looks off, everyone can see it — check the Leaderboard tab.

Tiebreaker

If the weekend ends in a tie, the tiebreaker is decided at Christian's discretion. Don't bother lobbying for it ahead of time.

If Something Breaks

If the app doesn't log your drink, come find Christian and he'll log it manually.

How scoring works

Each of the 5 games is its own contest. Whoever's room drinks the most of that game's drink by the end of the weekend wins it. The Swim-Up Bar Circuit counts each of the 5 bars separately. If the whole weekend ends in a tie, Maria picks a day — whichever tied room drank most that day wins. Still tied? She picks another day.
